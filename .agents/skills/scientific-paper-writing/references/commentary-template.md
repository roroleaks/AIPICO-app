# Scientific Commentary Template (Obstetrics & Gynecology)

Use this canonical structure when authoring a Step 4 Scientific Commentary Paper.

---

```markdown
# [Title: Informative Clinical Question Synthesis]

**Keywords:** [5-6 MeSH keywords separated by commas]

---

### Abstract

**Background:** [Prevalence, clinical urgency, and problem statement (50-70 words)]
**Clinical Question:** [Formulated PICO question: In [Population], how does [Intervention] compare with [Comparator] regarding [Selected Outcomes]? (30-40 words)]
**Evidence Synthesis:** [Key findings from randomized trials and meta-analyses, reporting quantitative effect sizes (RR, OR, 95% CI) (100-140 words) with Vancouver citations [1, 2]]
**Conclusion:** [Decisive clinical takeaway, practice recommendation, and GRADE certainty (40-60 words)]

---

### Introduction

[Paragraph 1: Disease epidemiology, clinical impact of preterm birth or obstetric condition, neonatal morbidity burdens.]

[Paragraph 2: Biological plausibility, pharmacological mechanisms of the intervention, and physiological rationale for the comparator.]

[Paragraph 3: Clinical dilemma, reason for clinical equipoise, recent trial developments, and precise objective of this commentary [1].]

---

### Discussion

#### Primary Efficacy Outcomes
[Synthesize trial data evaluating the primary clinical endpoint, e.g. delivery < 34 weeks, live birth rate [1, 2]. Detail absolute and relative risk reductions.]

#### Secondary Maternal and Neonatal Outcomes
[Synthesize secondary outcomes: composite neonatal morbidity, respiratory distress syndrome, intraventricular hemorrhage, NICU stay duration, maternal tolerability [2, 3].]

#### Subgroup Stratification & Clinical Nuances
[Examine effectiveness across clinically relevant subgroups: prior preterm birth history vs unselected, cervical length severity (< 15 mm vs 15-24 mm), multifetal pregnancies.]

#### Adverse Events and Procedural Risks
[Contrast safety profiles: non-invasive pharmacological therapy vs invasive surgical cerclage risks (bleeding, anesthesia, membrane rupture, infection) [1, 4].]

#### Quality of Evidence and Methodological Appraisal
[Appraise certainty of evidence using GRADE criteria, risk of bias in included trials, allocation concealment, and blinding feasibility.]

#### Controversies and Research Gaps
[Highlight conflicting trial findings, unanswered clinical questions, and ongoing clinical investigations.]

---

### Conclusion

[Paragraph 1: Clear, unambiguous clinical take-home message summarizing first-line recommendation for clinical practice.]

[Paragraph 2: Translational research priorities, need for individualized risk stratification, and patient counseling pearls.]

---

### References (Vancouver Style)

2. [Author BB, et al. Title. Journal. Year. doi/URL]
3. [Author CC, et al. Title. Journal. Year. doi/URL]
4. [Author DD, et al. Title. Journal. Year. doi/URL]
```
